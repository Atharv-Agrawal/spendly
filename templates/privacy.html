Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter into the app.
  • Usage data — basic information about how you interact with the Service, such as pages visited and actions taken.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive personal data beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data we collect solely to provide and improve the Service:

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To display your expense history, summaries, and category breakdowns.
  • To diagnose technical issues and improve app performance.
  • To send essential service communications (e.g., password reset emails).

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at support@spendly.app. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Google Fonts — to load the typefaces used in the interface. Google may log font requests; see Google's privacy policy for details.

We do not integrate analytics platforms, advertising networks, or social media trackers.

5. Cookies

Spendly uses a single session cookie to keep you logged in. This cookie is essential for the Service to function and is deleted when you log out or close your browser. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out at support@spendly.app.
